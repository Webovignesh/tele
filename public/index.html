How to get api_id / api_hash
  1. Log in to my.telegram.org with your Telegram account.
  2. Open API development tools.
  3. Fill in the app title and short name (anything, e.g. "TeleScraper").
  4. You get your api_id (a number) and api_hash (a hex string). Paste them above.
